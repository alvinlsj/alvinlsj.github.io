Why clients stay

Three things we put in writing

Most people leave an accountant over a surprise invoice or an unreturned call. These are the two things we removed.

01

Fixed fee, agreed first

You will know the accounting fee before the work begins. No hidden extras, and no unpleasant surprise at the end of the year when the return is done.

02

Quick calls are free

If something comes up mid-year and you need a two-minute answer, ring us. We have never charged a client for a short phone query and we are not about to start.

At least one face-to-face meeting every year to review how the business is tracking, plan next year's tax, and talk through vehicles, equipment, cash flow or a change of structure.
