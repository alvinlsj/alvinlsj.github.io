About

Small practice, on purpose.

NumberPad was founded in 2016 to do one thing properly: look after small business owners who want an accountant they can actually reach. Accounting is built on numbers, but the work is really built on knowing the person on the other side of them.

We support businesses from the very beginning — the point where you are still deciding whether to be a sole trader or a company — right through the years that follow. The core of it is listening carefully enough to understand what you are actually trying to build.

We meet every client face to face at least once a year. Not to sell anything — to look at how the year went and plan the next one.

Those meetings are where the useful conversations happen: whether to lease the van or buy it, how cash flow is tracking, what next year's tax bill looks like, whether the structure you set up three years ago still fits. And if something urgent comes up in between, a quick phone call is free. It always has been.

How we work

Three commitments

Fixed fees

You will know the fee before we start. No hidden charges, no surprise invoice at the end of the year when the return lands.

Free quick calls

Short questions are never billed. If you are unsure about something mid-year, ring — that is the whole point of having an accountant.

A yearly sit-down

At least one face-to-face meeting a year to review the business, plan for tax, and talk through anything that has changed.

Services

What we look after

From a single individual return through to medium-sized enterprises. If your business does not fit neatly into a package, we will build one around it.

01

Accounting & tax

Income tax returns for any entity, financial statements, capital gains tax, GST advice, and every registration you need — TFN, ABN, GST, PAYGW. Australian tax law shifts every year; keeping up with it is our job, not yours.

02

Bookkeeping

BAS and IAS, payroll, reconciliations, accounts payable and receivable. We work in Xero, MYOB and QuickBooks — and we will train you on Xero if you would rather keep it in house. Engage us for a couple of hours a week, a quarter, or a year.

03

Business set-up

Sole trader, partnership, family trust or company. Too many owners inherit a structure because it is what a friend did. We explain the trade-offs and get it right on day one, which is far cheaper than fixing it later.

04

Self-managed super

Your retirement fund, kept compliant with the SIS Act. To preserve independence the audit goes to a separate Brisbane auditor, and the audit fee is already inside our package price.

05

Tax planning

Run in the last quarter of the financial year so you know your estimated position and have an action plan before 30 June — rather than finding out in October.
